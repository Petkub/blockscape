Blockscape

Field manual

The world is a grid of numbers

Blockscape is a box of space cut into cubes. Every cube is one block, 1 metre on each side. The game stores one number per cube: 0 means air, 1 is grass, 36 is stone. Breaking a block writes a 0. Placing a block writes its number. Doors, stairs, slabs and torches also store a few extra bits for which way they face and whether they are open.

512 × 512blocks across, east–west by north–south
200blocks tall, from bedrock at 0 to the sky limit
52 millioncells, each holding one block number
20game ticks per second for hunger, furnaces and effects

A seed decides everything

The seed you type (letters, numbers and hyphens) is turned into a single 32-bit number, shown on the title screen as the seed number. That number starts a pseudo-random generator that always produces the same sequence, so the same seed rebuilds the same world, tree for tree.

Generation runs in this order:

  1. Biomes. Biome centres on a loose 12 × 12 grid. Each column belongs to its nearest centre, and each biome always gets at least two.
  2. Height. Each biome has its own smooth noise. Near borders the heights blend, so you get beaches and slopes.
  3. Layers. Bedrock, deepslate, stone, then a skin of grass, sand or sea floor.
  4. Underground. Veins of coal, iron, copper, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald, and worm-shaped caves.
  5. Life. Seven kinds of tree, flowers, cacti, sugar cane, kelp, coral, and finally the animals.

How the picture is drawn

The world is cut into chunks of 16 × 16 × 16. For each chunk the game only builds faces that touch air or see-through blocks; everything hidden is skipped. Corners are darkened by nearby blocks (ambient occlusion). Glowing blocks like torches, lava and glowstone stay bright at night. Changing a block rebuilds only the chunks around it. Only chunks within your render distance are built at all; more stream in as you move.

How things move

You are a box 0.6 wide and 1.8 tall. Each frame you move along Y, then X, then Z, and stop flush against the first solid box in the way. You step up 0.6 blocks automatically, so slabs and stairs need no jumping. Gravity is 28 blocks per second squared. Mobs follow simple rules: wander, flee, or chase you if they are hostile.

In inventory screens

  • Left click picks up or puts down a whole stack. Right click takes half, or puts down one.
  • Shift-click moves a stack straight to the other side.
  • Hover a slot and press 1–9 to swap it with that hotbar slot.

Survival

You have 10 hearts and 10 hunger points. Blocks take time to mine and drop items, and some need the right tool (stone needs a pickaxe; iron needs a stone pickaxe; diamond needs iron). You craft everything yourself. Hostile mobs come out at night and in dark caves. Fall damage, drowning, fire, lava, cactus and starvation can hurt you. When you die you drop everything and respawn at your bed or the world spawn.

Creative

You can't be hurt and you never get hungry. Double-tap Space to fly. Blocks break instantly (swords can't break blocks, like in vanilla). Press E for the creative inventory: every block, item and enchanted book, in tabs or by search. Hostile mobs ignore you.

Spectator

You fly through everything and can't touch anything. The hotbar, hearts, hunger, crosshair and info panel all disappear, so only the view is left. Scroll the mouse wheel to change flying speed, from 0.1× to 10×.

Switching

Choose a mode on the title screen, press the Game mode button in the pause menu, or type /gamemode creative (or /gamemode s, /gamemode sp).

Health

Hearts sit above the left end of the hotbar. Each heart is 2 health points. Golden hearts above them are absorption, extra health from a golden apple that is lost first. Your armour bar sits above the hearts.

Hunger

Drumsticks sit above the right end of the hotbar. Walking, swimming, jumping, fighting and especially sprinting use up hunger. With 9 or more drumsticks you slowly heal. At 3 or fewer you can't sprint. At zero you start to starve. Eating restores hunger and saturation, a hidden reserve that is used before the drumsticks go down.

Getting food

Kill animals for meat (cows drop beef, pigs porkchops, sheep mutton, chickens chicken, rabbits rabbit), catch fish with a fishing rod, or pick apples from oak leaves. Cook raw meat in a furnace: it restores about twice as much. Raw chicken and rotten flesh can give you Hunger; pufferfish poison you.

Ways to get hurt

  • Falling: 1 damage per block after the first 3. Land in water, on slime or on hay to stop it. A water bucket placed just before you land saves you, as in vanilla.
  • Drowning: bubbles appear above the hunger bar under water. When they run out you take 2 damage a second.
  • Fire and lava: you keep burning after you step out. Jump in water to put it out.
  • Mobs: zombies, husks, drowned, skeletons, spiders, cave spiders, creepers, endermen, witches, slimes, phantoms, pillagers, and wolves, bees or dolphins you've angered.
  • Cactus, magma blocks, suffocating in blocks and starving.

Smelting

Put raw ore, sand, raw meat or logs into the top slot of a furnace and fuel (coal, charcoal, wood, a lava bucket) into the bottom slot. One item takes 10 seconds; smokers cook food and blast furnaces smelt ore twice as fast. Taking the result gives you experience.

Sleeping

Right click a bed at night to skip to morning. It also sets your respawn point. You can't sleep with monsters nearby.

Attack cooldown

After every swing, a small bar under the crosshair refills. Hitting before it's full does much less damage. Swords recharge in 0.6 seconds, axes in about 1 second, the mace in 1.7 seconds. Placing blocks has a 0.2-second delay and mining the next block a 0.25-second delay, as in vanilla.

Critical hits

Attack while falling (after a jump) with a fully charged swing for 50% more damage and a burst of sparks. Sprinting into a hit knocks the target further instead.

Shields

Hold a shield in either hand and hold right click to raise it. It blocks every hit from in front, including arrows and explosions, while slowing you down.

Mace

Base damage 6. Hit something while falling and you do a smash attack: +4 damage per block for the first 3 blocks fallen, +2 per block for the next 5, then +1 per block. The smash throws up a ring of dust and a shockwave that knocks back everything nearby, and you take no fall damage. Density adds more damage per block, Breach pierces armour, and Wind Burst launches you back into the air for another smash. It has a long cooldown.

Spear

The spear's damage grows with your speed: multiplied by 1 + speed ÷ 5 blocks per second. Standing still it barely hurts; sprinting doubles it; diving with an elytra can multiply it seven times. Hold right click to charge, and anything you run into while moving fast gets hit automatically. Lunge dashes you forward when you jab. It reaches 4.5 blocks instead of 3.

Golden apples

A golden apple gives Regeneration II for 5 seconds and Absorption (2 golden hearts) for 2 minutes. The enchanted golden apple gives Regeneration II for 20 seconds, Absorption IV, Resistance and Fire Resistance for 5 minutes. You can eat them even when full.

Elytra

Wear the elytra in your chestplate slot. Jump off something high, then press Space in mid-air to open your wings. Look down to dive and gain speed, look up to trade speed for height, exactly like vanilla's physics. Right click a firework rocket while gliding for a boost. Crashing into a wall at speed hurts. The elytra wears down by 1 for each second of flight.

Other weapons

  • Bow: hold right click to draw (1 second for full power), release to shoot. Needs arrows unless you have Infinity or are in Creative.
  • Axes hit harder than swords but recharge slowly.
  • Totem of Undying: hold it and fatal damage leaves you on half a heart with Regeneration and Absorption.
  • Ender pearl: throw to teleport where it lands (costs 2.5 hearts).
  • Armour: leather, iron and diamond, each with helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Right click armour to wear it.

How to craft

Press E for your inventory: its 2×2 grid makes small things. Right click a crafting table for a 3×3 grid. Arrange items in the shape of a recipe and the result appears on the right. Shift-click the result to craft as many as you can.

To start in Survival: punch a tree for logs, turn logs into planks, planks into sticks and a crafting table, then make a wooden pickaxe. Stone tools come next, then a furnace to smelt iron.

Enchanted books and anvils

Enchantments are separate items: enchanted books. Put a weapon, tool or armour piece on the left of an anvil and a book on the right, and the anvil adds the book's enchantments for some experience levels. Two books (or two items) with the same enchantment at the same level combine into the next level. Anvils also repair items and rename them.

Get books from the enchanting table (put a plain book in it), from fishing, from the creative inventory, or with /give @s enchanted_book[enchantments={sharpness:5}].

The enchanting table

Put an item and lapis lazuli in the table and pick one of three offers. Bookshelves within two blocks unlock stronger offers (15 gives the best). Each offer costs 1 to 3 levels and lapis.

All enchantments

Orbital cannon shots

Six shots from Unstable SMP. Each one looks like an ordinary fishing rod and has 1 durability. Hold it, look at a block (up to 512 blocks away) and right click: you cast and pull the line in one motion, the shot breaks, and half a second later the payload arrives on that block. They can't be crafted. Take them from the creative inventory (Combat tab) or use /give.

Playing together

Blockscape has a dedicated server, like Minecraft's. It runs the world (mobs, items, explosions, furnaces, time and weather) and everyone on the same Wi-Fi plays in it from their browser.

  1. On the computer that will host, double-click Start Server.command in the blockscape folder (or run node server.js). Node.js must be installed.
  2. The window shows addresses like http://192.168.1.20:25565. Open that in a browser on each computer, or type the address on the Multiplayer tab.
  3. Enter your name and press Join server.

Server settings

Edit server.properties and restart the server: server-port, level-name, level-seed (empty for a random one), gamemode, difficulty, max-players, pvp, motd, autosave-seconds. The world and every player's inventory are saved in worlds/<level-name>/level.json every minute and when you stop the server with Ctrl+C.

Using commands

Press / to start a command, or T then type one. Suggestions appear as you type; Tab fills them in. Coordinates accept ~ for "where I am", so /tp ~ ~30 ~ lifts you 30 blocks. Type /? in game for the full list.

Shortcuts for tedious tasks

  • /fill ~-5 ~-1 ~-5 ~5 ~-1 ~5 stone_bricks lays a floor in one go.
  • /fill ~-3 ~ ~-3 ~3 ~4 ~3 glass hollow builds a glass room.
  • /give @s diamond_sword[enchantments={sharpness:5,fire_aspect:2}]
  • /locate biome desert then click the coordinates to go there.
  • /time set day, /weather clear, /kill @e[type=zombie]
